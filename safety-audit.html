A Clear, Structured Approach

Areas We Inspect

What a Safety Audit Covers

Our audits assess the core safety areas that matter most for people and property.

Fire Safety

Fire detection, suppression systems, exits and emergency readiness.

Electrical Safety

Wiring, load, earthing and electrical hazard assessment.

Structural Safety

Structural condition, access routes and building integrity checks.

Security Systems

CCTV coverage, access control and surveillance evaluation.

Crowd & Occupancy

Occupancy limits, crowd flow and emergency evacuation planning.

Compliance Review

Alignment with applicable regulatory and statutory safety requirements.

Reporting

What You Receive

001

002

003

Recommendations

004

Support

Guidance on implementing improvements and maintaining ongoing safety.
